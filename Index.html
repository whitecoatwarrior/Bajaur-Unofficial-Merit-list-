About This Merit List

This merit list represents the current standing of Bajaur region students based on their MDCAT scores and HEC aggregates. The list is compiled from available data and is subject to change as more results are announced.

Important Notes:

  • This is an unofficial merit list compiled from available sources
  • Final merit positions will be determined by the official HEC authorities
  • Students are advised to verify their rankings through official channels
  • MDCAT scores and aggregates are as reported by students
  • All students in this list have FATA In status

For accurate and official merit positions, please contact the Higher Education Commission (HEC) Pakistan or your respective educational board.
